Good questions

Pricing, answered.

How do you count my followers?
We read your connected account’s follower count straight from Instagram. You’re always on the tier that matches your current reach. No manual upgrades, no awkward overage emails.
What happens when I cross a tier?
Congrats first. 💚 When you grow past your tier’s ceiling, we move you up to the next one and prorate the difference for the rest of the month. Nothing breaks mid-flow.
Is there really no per-message charge?
Correct. Send 100 DMs or 100,000, your monthly price doesn’t move. That’s the whole point. You pay for reach, not for doing your job well.
Do all tiers get every feature?
All paid tiers get every feature: Hinglish detection, comment-to-DM, story flows, chatbots, broadcasts, lead capture, advanced triggers and analytics. The Free tier covers the core automation but excludes lead-capture flows, broadcasts and gated-link features like "ask for follow" or "ask for email".
Can I cancel anytime?
Anytime, in one tap, no “are you sure” maze. Your automations pause and your data stays exportable for 30 days.
I have over 5 million followers.
Then let’s build something for you. Custom pricing on the 5M+ tier comes with priority infrastructure, a dedicated contact and team onboarding. Hit “Let’s talk”.
