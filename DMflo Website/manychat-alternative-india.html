02 · Language

They don’t speak how your audience writes.

This is the real gap, and it’s bigger than price.

Your comments section isn’t in English. It’s “price kitna hai”, “COD hai kya”, “delivery Bangalore mein hoti hai?”, “enna price sollunga”. Hindi in Roman script, Tamil in Roman script, English grammar with Hindi words dropped in mid-sentence. Nobody switches to clean English to ask you a question.

Tools built for American audiences see this as unmatched noise. DMflo reads it as what it is: a customer asking to buy something.

03 · Mobile

They assume you have a laptop.

India is a phone-first country. Most creators here have never opened their automation tool on a desktop, because there isn’t one.

Global tools don’t work that way. ManyChat has an app, but by their own documentation it’s for managing things on the go. There’s no analytics view in it, new flow creation is limited to Instagram flows, and you can’t configure integrations, import contacts, manage roles, change billing or even reconnect your Instagram account from it. Something will eventually send you looking for a laptop.

DMflo’s app is the whole product. Build your automation in it: goals, voice, agents, the full setup in about two minutes. Then watch your numbers in it, live: click-through, accounts reached, what converted. There’s a full web app for agencies and teams, but you’ll never need it to run your own account.

Good questions

For Indian creators, answered.

Is there a ManyChat alternative in Indian rupees?
Yes. DMflo is priced in rupees: ₹0 free, ₹599/month Pro (₹499 billed yearly), ₹1,999/month Agentic (₹1,499 billed yearly), all flat with no per-contact billing.
Does DMflo understand Hindi and Hinglish?
Natively. Hindi in Devanagari, Hindi in Roman script, and Hinglish mixed with English mid-sentence. It also handles Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Malayalam, Kannada, Gujarati and Punjabi, and it replies in whichever script the person used.
What’s the cheapest Instagram automation tool for Indian creators?
DMflo has a free plan with real comment and DM automation, not a 25-contact trial. Paid starts at ₹499/month flat when billed yearly.
Can I run Instagram automation entirely from my phone?
With DMflo, yes. Setup, going live, analytics and changes all happen in the mobile app, in about two minutes. Most global tools have an app, but it’s a companion to a desktop dashboard; ManyChat’s documentation, for example, lists integrations, contact imports, roles, billing and account reconnection as web-only.
Do I need a business account?
An Instagram Creator or Business account, yes. That’s Meta’s requirement for messaging automation, not ours.
Is it safe?
Meta’s rules, the 24-hour messaging window, one private reply per comment, rate limits, are enforced inside the product. The agents can’t break them.
