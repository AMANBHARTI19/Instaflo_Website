2

The additional review Meta requires from companies that access other businesses’ Meta assets on their behalf — exactly what a DM automation tool does. Meta reviewed how DMflo handles your account access and approved it.

App Review — every permission individually approved

Each Instagram capability DMflo uses (reading comments, sending DM replies, managing messages) is a scoped permission that Meta’s review team approved after seeing exactly how we use it — including screen recordings of the product.

Every action DMflo performs goes through graph.instagram.com, Meta’s official endpoint, inside Meta’s published rate limits and messaging policies. There is no browser automation, no session hijacking, and no unofficial “private API” anywhere in our stack.

03 How connecting your Instagram actually works

Your password is never shared. Login happens on Instagram’s own page (OAuth). If any automation tool asks you to type your Instagram password into their app, close the tab — that’s the unsafe kind.

  1. You log in on Instagram, not on DMflo. Clicking “Connect Instagram” sends you to Instagram’s own login page. Your password is typed there — Meta’s servers — and DMflo never sees, stores, or transmits it.
  2. Instagram hands us a scoped access token. After you approve the listed permissions, Instagram issues DMflo a token limited to exactly those capabilities. It can send DM replies on your behalf; it cannot change your password, post content, or touch anything you didn’t approve.
  3. You stay in control, permanently. Revoke DMflo’s access anytime from Instagram’s settings (Apps and Websites) or by disconnecting inside DMflo — either kills the token instantly. Deleting your DMflo account erases your data per our privacy policy.

04 Why this matters for your account

Instagram bans accounts for using tools that fake human behavior — password-login bots that like, follow, and DM through a hidden browser. Official-API tools are the opposite category: Meta knows exactly who we are (verified twice over), reviewed what we do, and meters every call we make.

Using DMflo is using Instagram’s own professional messaging infrastructure — that’s the entire reason we built on it.

Meta’s messaging rules are baked in. The 24-hour messaging window, one private reply per comment — DMflo’s agents simply can’t break them, so you never think about compliance.

05 The questions people actually ask

Is DMflo approved by Meta?

Yes — in the three ways that exist for a messaging tool: Meta Business Verification, Tech Provider access verification, and App Review approval of every Instagram permission we use. “Meta-approved” claims without these are just marketing; ours are checkable.

Can using DMflo get my Instagram account banned?

Automation through the official API, within Meta’s rate limits and policies, is explicitly supported by Instagram for professional accounts — it’s what the API exists for. DMflo additionally enforces its own safety margins below Meta’s published limits. What gets accounts banned is password-login bots simulating humans — a different category of tool entirely.

Does DMflo see my Instagram password?

Never. Login happens on Instagram’s own page (OAuth). If any automation tool asks you to type your Instagram password into their app, close the tab — that’s the unsafe kind.

What permissions does DMflo get?

Only messaging-related scopes: reading comments and messages on your professional account and replying to them. Each was individually approved by Meta’s App Review. DMflo cannot post to your feed, follow/unfollow anyone, or modify your profile.

How do I verify any of this myself?

Connect an account and check Instagram’s Settings → Apps and Websites — you’ll see DMflo listed with its exact scopes, via Instagram’s official app system. Password-bots can’t appear there, because they never went through Meta at all.
