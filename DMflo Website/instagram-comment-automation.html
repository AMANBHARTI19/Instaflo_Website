The honest problem

Why most comment automation disappoints.

Not because keyword flows are bad. Because keyword flows on their own only ever cover a fraction of what people actually write.

01 · Coverage

Keyword flows only fire on the words you predicted.

Your “PRICE” flow is genuinely useful. When someone types the keyword, they get the exact reply you wrote, every time, and you want that predictability for your link, your code, your giveaway rule.

The problem is everything else. Someone types “how much”, or “price kitna hai”, or fat-fingers “prcie”, and on a keyword-only tool nothing happens. So you spend your evenings adding words to a list, and you still never catch them all.

DMflo keeps your flow exactly as you built it, and puts agents behind it to read what everything else means. You keep the control. The agents cover the rest.

Good questions

Comment automation, answered.

How do I auto-reply to Instagram comments?
Connect your Instagram Creator or Business account to a tool with Meta Comment API access, then set what you want to happen. With DMflo you get both layers: build keyword flows for the replies you want word-for-word, and set a goal so the agents handle everything the keywords don’t match.
How do I send a DM when someone comments?
That’s comment-to-DM. Someone comments a keyword and the tool sends them a private reply with your link, exactly as you wrote it. In DMflo the agents also pick up the people who asked for it without using your keyword. Meta allows one private reply per comment, and DMflo enforces that automatically.
Is Instagram comment automation allowed?
Yes, through Meta’s official APIs, within their rules: a 24-hour messaging window, one private reply per comment, and rate limits. DMflo enforces all three so your account stays clean.
Is there a free Instagram comment automation tool?
DMflo’s free plan includes real comment and DM automation, comment-to-DM, story replies and any-language replies, not a 25-contact trial.
Will it work if my audience comments in another language?
Yes. DMflo replies in whatever language and script the person used, including mixes like Hinglish, Spanglish and Taglish. Nothing to configure.
Can it hide spam comments?
Yes. The moderation agent classifies every incoming comment and quietly removes spam and abuse.
