Straight answers

Questions people actually ask.

Is this just keyword automation with a new coat of paint?

No. Keyword tools fire a canned reply when a word matches, and dump the thinking on you. DMflo runs a team of agents that understand what a message means: they read intent, reply in your voice, hide spam, and steer conversations toward a sale or a captured lead. You set the goal; they work toward it.

Will it sound like a bot to my audience?

The engagement agent learns your tone, slang and emoji from how you actually write, and replies in the language your audience uses, Hinglish included. No generic templates.

What does “flat pricing” actually mean?

You pay the same price every month no matter how much you grow. No per-contact or per-message billing. Tools that charge per active contact quietly raise your bill as your audience grows. We think that’s a success tax, so we don’t charge it.

Will automation get my account flagged?

Meta’s rules (the 24-hour messaging window, one private reply per comment) are baked into the agents. They simply can’t break them, so you never think about compliance.

Does DMflo ever post as me?

Never. The agents reply to comments and DMs on your behalf. They don’t create posts, stories or anything public as you.

Is it only for Hinglish audiences?

English works great too. Hinglish is the edge no global tool has, not a limitation. More languages are on the way; the point is that the agents speak your audience’s language.
