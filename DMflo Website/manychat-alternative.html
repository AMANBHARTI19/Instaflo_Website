03 · Both layers

Keyword flows and agents, on one platform.

Keyword flows are good at what they’re good at. When you want an exact, predictable reply, a discount code, a link, a giveaway rule, you build the flow and it fires the same way every time. DMflo gives you that, and you stay in control of it.

What rule-based tools can’t do is everything else. Someone phrases the question differently and nothing happens, so you keep adding keywords to a list forever.

So DMflo puts four agents behind your flows. Your keywords handle the cases you predicted. The agents read intent and cover the rest, in your voice and in your audience’s language. You only step in for the conversations that genuinely need you.

04 · Mobile

You build it on your phone, and you watch it work on your phone.

To be fair: ManyChat has a mobile app. So do several tools here. That’s not the difference.

The difference is what you can do in it. In DMflo’s app you create the automation, goals, brand voice, every agent, the full setup, and then you watch your numbers in real time: click-through, accounts reached, which conversations converted. Live, in your hand.

ManyChat’s app has four tabs: Home, Live Chat, Contacts, Automation. There’s no analytics view. New flow creation is limited to Instagram flows, and per their own documentation integrations, contact imports, roles, billing and even reconnecting your Instagram account are web-only. Their guidance is to use the web version for anything advanced.

That’s a window onto a desktop product. It assumes you have a laptop. If you run your business from your phone, and most creators do, that’s the difference between a tool you set up once and forget, and one you actually adjust after every post.

Good questions

ManyChat vs DMflo, answered.

Is DMflo cheaper than ManyChat?
Usually, and the gap widens as you grow. DMflo Pro is ₹599/month (₹499 billed yearly) and Agentic is ₹1,999/month (₹1,499 billed yearly), flat, whatever your contact count. ManyChat’s Essential starts at $14/month for 250 contacts and scales up with your audience, with per-contact overages above plan limits. At small scale it’s close. At 5,000 contacts it isn’t.
Can I import my ManyChat contacts?
Yes. Export from ManyChat, import to DMflo. They’re your contacts.
Does DMflo do WhatsApp?
No. Instagram only. That focus is why setup takes two minutes instead of an afternoon, but if you need WhatsApp, ManyChat or Wati will serve you better and we’d rather say so.
Does ManyChat have a mobile app?
Yes, on iOS and Android. But it’s a companion to their web dashboard. It has four tabs (Home, Live Chat, Contacts, Automation) and no analytics view, new flow creation is limited to Instagram flows, and per ManyChat’s own documentation integrations, contact imports, user roles, channel reconnection (their “Refresh Permissions” feature) and billing are web-only.
Can I create automations from the DMflo app?
Yes, and it’s the full setup, not a cut-down version. Goals, brand voice, which agents handle what. About two minutes, no laptop involved.
Can I see analytics in the DMflo app?
Yes, in real time. Link click-through rate, unique accounts reached, and which conversations converted, live as it happens, not a weekly export from a desktop dashboard.
Is DMflo safe? Will it get my account flagged?
Meta’s messaging rules, the 24-hour window, one private reply per comment, rate limits, are enforced inside DMflo. The agents can’t break them. That’s our job to handle, not yours to remember.
Which languages does DMflo handle?
Any. If someone writes in Hindi, Spanish, Korean, Arabic, Portuguese or Tagalog, the agents reply in that language and that script. They also read the mixes real people actually type: Hinglish, Spanglish, Konglish, Tanglish, Taglish, Arabizi. Nothing to set up.
Is DMflo available now?
Yes, DMflo is live. Create your account and start free in about two minutes: the free plan is free forever and no card is needed.
