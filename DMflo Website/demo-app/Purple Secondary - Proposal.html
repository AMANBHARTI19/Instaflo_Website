Design system · exploration

Purple as a limited tertiary accent

Agreed — purple as a full secondary was too loud. So here it’s demoted to a tertiary accent: lime stays the one primary color, warm white and off-white carry everything else, and purple only appears in a handful of tiny, semantic moments. Its single job is to mark AI & smart intelligence — the one thing lime (which means “automation / action”) shouldn’t also stand for. Never a surface, button, or message bubble.

The rule

Lime = action & automation and stays the loudest color on every screen. Purple = AI intelligence, appearing only as tiny tags, dots, one insight marker, or a single chart highlight. If a screen has more than one or two purple marks, it’s too much.
