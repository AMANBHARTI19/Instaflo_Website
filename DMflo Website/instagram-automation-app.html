The honest bit

Why other apps don’t do this.

Most tools in this category have a mobile app. That’s not the issue. The issue is what the app is for.

Almost all of them are companions to a desktop dashboard: watch your automation run, read your inbox, fire off a quick reply. The moment you want to change how something works or understand how it’s performing, you’re opening a laptop.

ManyChat documents this openly

Four tabs. No analytics view.

Their app has four tabs: Home, Live Chat, Contacts, Automation. There’s no analytics view. And their own help pages list what’s web-only:

  • New flow creation is limited to Instagram flows
  • Integrations can’t be accessed or configured
  • You can’t refresh permissions, meaning you can’t reconnect your Instagram account
  • Contact imports and deletions aren’t available
  • User roles, account settings and channel management are desktop-only
  • Billing changes require the web version

Their own recommendation is to use the web version for anything advanced. That’s honest of them, and it’s fine, if you have a desk.

Most creators don’t. They have a phone and an hour between shoots.

The point

Why this changes what automation is worth.

A tool you can only configure at a desk is a tool you configure once and then mostly ignore. It drifts. Your offer changes, your voice changes, your audience changes, and the automation keeps saying what it said in January.

A tool that lives in your pocket is one you actually adjust: after a Reel does unexpectedly well, when you launch something, when a reply reads wrong. That’s the difference between automation that decays and automation that improves.

Good questions

The app, answered.

Can I set up Instagram automation from my phone?
With DMflo, yes. The entire setup happens in the mobile app and takes about two minutes. Many tools require a desktop for initial configuration even when they offer a mobile app.
Does ManyChat have a mobile app?
Yes, on iOS and Android. Per ManyChat’s own documentation it’s built for managing automation on the go: new flow creation is limited to Instagram flows, and integrations, contact imports, user roles, channel reconnection and billing are web-only.

Nothing you’d need to run your own Instagram. The web app exists for teams and agencies, covering multi-account management, deeper analytics and CRM configuration, but a solo creator never has to open it.
Is there a web version too?
