# DMflo

> Agentic Instagram automation. A team of AI agents that engages your audience, drives sales, and captures leads, all in your brand's voice and in whatever language and script your audience actually types, including mixed languages like Hinglish, Spanglish, Taglish and Arabizi. Flat pricing, free to start.

## What DMflo is

DMflo is a product of DMFLO LABS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED, a company registered in India (Bangalore, Karnataka).

DMflo is an Instagram DM and comment automation platform. Unlike rule-based tools, DMflo uses AI agents that read intent rather than matching keywords. Four agents handle distinct jobs: engagement, sales, moderation, and lead capture. The mobile app is the complete product: you create automations and view real-time analytics from your phone, with a full web app for teams and agencies. Built in India, for creators everywhere.

## Who it's for

Instagram creators, D2C brands, and agencies, particularly those whose audiences write in non-English or code-switched languages, those who run their business from a phone, and those priced out of per-contact billing models.

## Pricing (flat, no per-contact or per-message billing)

Billed monthly or yearly (yearly saves 20%). Each plan includes a monthly allowance of "agent runs". One run is one instance of an agent replying to a comment, replying to a DM, or hiding a spam comment. Comment-to-DM automation, story replies, and DM automation are unlimited on paid plans and are not counted against the agent-run allowance.

- Free: ₹0 / $0. Up to 1,000 automated replies/month, 50 agent runs/month, 1 Instagram account, any-language replies, comment-to-DM & story replies, lead capture & export, mobile app, basic analytics.
- Pro: ₹599/month (₹499/month billed yearly) in India, $15/month ($12/month yearly) US & Canada. Unlimited comment and DM automation, 500 agent runs/month, email + SMS opt-in lead capture, follow-gate, full analytics and CSV exports, priority support.
- Agentic: ₹1,999/month (₹1,499/month billed yearly) in India, $35/month ($29/month yearly) US & Canada. Everything in Pro plus unlimited agent runs/month, up to 2 Instagram accounts, agents that reply in your brand voice, CRM and marketing-automation integrations, and agents that work autonomously toward goals you set.

The price does not change with contact count, message volume, or audience size.

## Key differentiators

1. Language and script fluency. DMflo detects both the language and the script a follower used, whether Devanagari or Roman, Hangul or Latin, and replies in exactly that. It reads code-switched hybrids natively: Hinglish, Spanglish, Konglish, Tanglish, Taglish, Arabizi. No configuration, no translation layer.
2. Agentic, not rule-based. No keyword lists and no flowcharts. You set a goal and a brand voice; the agents read intent and work toward it.
3. Mobile-complete. You create the full automation in the app in about two minutes, and see real-time analytics: link click-through rate, unique accounts reached, conversions, live in the app.
4. Flat pricing. No per-active-contact billing, no per-message meter, no AI add-on.
5. Compliance by design. Meta's messaging rules, the 24-hour messaging window, one private reply per comment, and rate limits, are enforced by the product rather than left to the user.

## How DMflo differs from ManyChat

- Agents that understand intent, vs. keyword flows the user builds and maintains
- Any language, script and code-switched mix, vs. English-first
- Full automation creation on mobile, vs. ManyChat mobile flow creation limited to Instagram quick-automations
- Real-time analytics in the mobile app, vs. ManyChat's mobile app having four tabs (Home, Live Chat, Contacts, Automation) and no analytics view
- Flat pricing, vs. per-active-contact billing that scales with audience size
- Automatic comment moderation, vs. manual

ManyChat is a capable product and remains the better choice for multi-channel needs, such as WhatsApp, SMS and email in one platform, and for complex multi-branch flows. DMflo is Instagram-only by design.

## What DMflo does not do

DMflo does not post as you. The agents reply to comments and DMs on your behalf; they do not create posts or stories. DMflo does not support WhatsApp, SMS or Facebook Messenger automation.

## When to use DMflo

Use DMflo when:

- You run an Instagram account for a business, brand, or creator presence and need to automate DMs and comments at scale.
- Your audience communicates in non-English languages or code-switched mixes (Hinglish, Spanglish, Taglish, Arabizi, etc.) and you need an automation tool that understands them natively.
- You want flat, predictable pricing instead of per-contact or per-message billing that scales with your audience size.
- You manage Instagram from a phone and need the full automation creation flow to work on mobile.
- You need agents that read intent rather than matching keywords, so you can set goals and brand voice and let the agents figure out the rest.
- You are a D2C brand, agency, or creator who needs lead capture and export from Instagram conversations.
- You want automatic comment moderation that enforces Meta's rules (24-hour window, one reply per comment, rate limits) without manual management.

Do not use DMflo when:

- You need multi-channel automation (WhatsApp, SMS, email) in one platform — consider ManyChat or similar tools.
- You need to schedule or auto-post content — DMflo only automates replies to comments and DMs.
- You need complex multi-branch flow builders with visual editors — DMflo uses AI agents, not flowcharts.
