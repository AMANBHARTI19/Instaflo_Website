Good questions

Pricing, answered.

What is an agent run?
An agent run is one instance of an agent doing work for you: replying to a comment, handling a DM, or hiding a spam comment. One action by one agent, one run. Free includes 50 agent runs a month, Pro 500, and Agentic unlimited. Comment → DM automation, story replies and DM automation don’t count against your agent runs, they stay unlimited on paid plans.
What happens if I use all my agent runs?
Your automations keep running. The agents pause their autonomous work until your next monthly reset, and we’ll tell you well before you get there so nothing goes quiet mid-campaign. You can upgrade at any point and the new runs are available straight away.
What’s the difference between the three plans?
Free gets the agents automating your DMs and comments, enough to see real results, with 50 agent runs a month. Pro adds unlimited keyword automation, 500 agent runs and power features for engagement and growth. Agentic is the full platform: unlimited agent runs, email + SMS lead capture, CRM and marketing-automation integrations, and agents that work toward your goals autonomously.
Are the Free and Pro plans still “agentic”?
Yes, the same team of agents runs on every plan, understanding intent and replying in your voice. Higher tiers give them more autonomy, more reach, and integrations with the rest of your stack.
Is there really no per-message or per-contact charge?
Correct. We never bill per contact or per message, and your bill never moves with the size of your audience. Each plan includes a monthly allowance of agent runs, and that’s the only number to watch. Tools that bill per active contact quietly raise your bill as you grow. We think that’s a success tax, so we don’t charge it.
Who is the Agentic plan for?
Bigger creators, D2C brands and agencies who want to sell more to a highly engaged audience. Capture qualified leads with email + SMS opt-in, export intent to a CRM, and let the agents capture and convert while the team sleeps.
Can I start on Free and upgrade later?
That’s the recommended path. Get your first results on Free, flip to Pro when you want the caps gone, and move to Agentic when you’re ready for full autopilot. Everything carries over.
Can I cancel anytime?
Anytime, in one tap, no “are you sure” maze. Your automations pause and your data stays exportable for 30 days.
