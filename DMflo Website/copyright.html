05Reporting copyright infringement

We respect intellectual-property rights and expect our users to do the same. If you believe content available through DMflo infringes your copyright, send a written notice to aman@trydmflo.com including:

  1. Your contact details (name, address, email).
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim was infringed.
  3. Identification of the allegedly infringing material and where it is located.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised.
  5. A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights-holder or authorised to act on their behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We will review valid notices and may remove or disable access to the material, and terminate repeat infringers’ accounts.
