Connect it

Set it up in Claude or ChatGPT

Both take about a minute. All you need is the DMflo MCP URL.

  1. 1

    Open Connectors

    In Claude, go to Customize › Connectors. On desktop you can also press Ctrl+,; in the browser use your profile icon › Settings, then Connectors in the sidebar.

  2. 2

    Add a custom connector

    Click + (or Add), then Add custom connector. If Claude asks for a connector type, choose Web.

  3. 3

    Paste the DMflo URL

    Name it DMflo and paste https://api.trydmflo.com/mcp as the remote MCP server URL, then click Add.

  4. 4

    Connect and sign in

    Find DMflo in the Connectors list and click Connect to authorize it with your DMflo account.

  5. 5

    Turn it on in a chat

    Adding a connector doesn’t enable it everywhere. In a conversation, click + › Add connectors and toggle DMflo on.

Custom connectors work on Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise, though Free accounts get one custom connector. On Team and Enterprise an Owner adds it first under Organization settings › Connectors, and members then click Connect. Claude’s help article

  1. 1

    Turn on developer mode

    In ChatGPT on the web, open Settings from your profile menu, find the connectors or apps section, open Advanced settings and switch on Developer mode. Custom connectors only appear once it is on.

  2. 2

    Create a connector

    Back in the connectors list, click Create. The button only shows after developer mode is enabled.

  3. 3

    Fill in the details

    Name it DMflo, add a short description, and paste https://api.trydmflo.com/mcp as the MCP server URL. Choose OAuth as the authentication method.

  4. 4

    Authorize

    ChatGPT warns you that custom connectors run third-party code on your behalf. Accept, then sign in with your DMflo account.

  5. 5

    Enable it in a chat

    Open the tools menu in a conversation and toggle DMflo on. Your agent can now call the DMflo tools.

Developer mode is available on Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise/Edu, not on Free. On Business and Enterprise an admin enables it first under Workspace Settings › Permissions & Roles. OpenAI has renamed these screens more than once, so if the paths above don’t match, look for Advanced settings at the bottom of the connectors or apps page. OpenAI’s help article
