The edge · Language

Any language. Any script. Even the mixed ones.

Whatever language a comment or DM comes in, the agents answer in that same one. Spanish in, Spanish out. Devanagari in, Devanagari out. And when your audience blends two languages into one sentence, the way real people actually type, DMflo reads the hybrid natively.

  • Detects language and script, then mirrors both
  • Native on hybrids: Hinglish, Spanglish, Konglish, Tanglish, Taglish
  • Handles slang, typos, short forms and emoji
  • No language rules or translation step to configure
