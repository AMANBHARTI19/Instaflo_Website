Good questions

Pricing, answered.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free gives you the core automation toolkit — up to 1,000 DMs a month, Hinglish detection, comment-to-DM, story flows, a basic chatbot, lead capture and basic analytics. Pro unlocks everything with no caps: unlimited DMs, unlimited automations, follow-gating, advanced chatbots, broadcasts, full analytics, CSV exports and priority support. One flat price, no surprises.
Is there really no per-message charge?
Correct. Send 100 DMs or 100,000, your monthly price doesn't move. That's the whole point. You pay a flat ₹499 on Pro and that's it — no per-contact, no per-message billing.
What happens when I hit 1,000 DMs on Free?
Your automations pause for the rest of that calendar month. Your data and flows stay intact. Upgrade to Pro anytime and they'll resume immediately with no cap in sight.
Can I cancel anytime?
Anytime, in one tap, no "are you sure" maze. Your automations pause and your data stays exportable for 30 days. Drop back to Free and your core flows still work.
Do you offer team or agency plans?
Pro already covers creators, brands and agencies. If you're managing a large portfolio of accounts or need custom onboarding, reach out via the waitlist and we'll sort you out.
